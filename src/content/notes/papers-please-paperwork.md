## The core verb

- Stamp approve / deny. A binary, bureaucratic action.
- Why a *stamp* specifically: it is impersonal and quick, and it signals authority.

## How the systems carry the theme

| Mechanic | Meaning | Feeling |
|---|---|---|
| Daily salary vs. family expenses | Survival depends on compliance | Pressure to obey |
| Growing rulebook | The state becomes more absurd | Fatigue and distrust |
| Penalty citations | Mercy has a price | Guilt when being kind |

- Story beats are cheap when the *economy* already argues the theme.
