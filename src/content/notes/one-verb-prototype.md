*Outline for you to fill in. Small experiments like this show how you think, even when they never turn into full games.*

## The constraint

Pick one verb (e.g. *wait*, *push*, *let go*) and build a 5-minute experience where it is the only input.

## What I built

- Verb chosen and why.
- Three moments where the same verb means something different.

## What I learned

- Did context alone change the meaning of the verb?
- What would I need to add to make it land harder?
